What the colours and marks mean

  • Grey No state scheme listed in the compendium. Schemes open across India still apply.
  • Light green 1–2 state schemes or trusts listed
  • Medium green 3–4 listed
  • Dark green 5 or more listed
  • Orange dot MOHAN Foundation has an office in this state
  • Yellow outline The state you have selected
